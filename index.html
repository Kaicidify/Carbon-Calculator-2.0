Carbon Footprint Calculator

Climate change is real!!

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, especially carbon dioxide, that are produced by our daily activities such as driving, using electricity, eating and shopping. These gases trap heat in the atmosphere and are the main cause of global warming and climate change.

Find out how big your carbon footprint is!

Every small action counts. By understanding your impact, you can make better choices to protect our planet for future generations.
